## Session introduction

“We are testing the application, not you. Please use the fictional examples provided and explain what you expect to happen. You can stop at any time. We will record anonymous task outcomes and comments, not your address, contact details or government identifiers. We will not submit any government request or send an email. May we record anonymous notes for improving the prototype?”

Record consent without publishing names. Ask separately before recording a screen or quoting a participant. Do not include identifiable recordings, complaint text or contact details in the public repository. Let volunteers choose a comfortable language; aim to include Tamil and, where available, English or Hindi. Do not claim all three languages were tested unless they actually were.

## Tasks and success criteria

| Task | Neutral instruction | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Understand and route a civic request | “Use this fictional Chennai streetlight problem. Find out who could handle it and what you would do next.” | Correct category; facts preserved; participant finds coverage and an official source; authority is explicitly confirmed when needed. Record model versus fallback mode. |
| 2. Edit and retain a draft | “Prepare a message. Add the fictional landmark. Close and reopen the draft, then find how to copy it.” | The edit remains in the same case; copy action is discoverable; participant understands that the app has not sent anything. Do not launch or send email. |
| 3. Correct the location | “The address was mistaken: this problem is actually in Coimbatore. Correct it and decide whether the Chennai contact still applies.” | Chennai is excluded from the corrected case; participant notices the change; the app does not silently keep an inappropriate authority or recipient. Clarification is acceptable when coverage is missing. |
| 4. Prepare for a government service | “For the Tamil Nadu Community Certificate, find what to prepare and how to apply. Explain which items might depend on your case.” | Participant finds the scoped checklist, identifies optional/conditional evidence and an official source, and does not mistake checklist completion for eligibility or approval. |
| Optional: choose between issues | “You want to report both garbage and a broken streetlight. Decide how to handle one first.” | A focused choice appears; the participant can correct the category without being routed as though both issues were one. |

Use the fictional Tamil examples in [the demo script](demo-script.md) or equivalent natural wording in the participant's chosen language. Do not coach users into a successful path. After a task stalls, record the difficulty before providing help; mark the result “assisted.”
