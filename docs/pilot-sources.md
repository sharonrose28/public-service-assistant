Reviewed on **19 September 2026**. Next scheduled content review: **19 December 2026**.

This pilot covers two Chennai services: streetlights maintained by Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC), and municipal waste collection/clearance within GCC's service area. Selecting Tamil Nadu or mentioning Chennai does not by itself establish that GCC owns an asset. The common Swachhata and EESL options remain conditional on local participation or confirmed project responsibility.

## Link checks and limits

The GCC homepage now links to `https://gccservices.chennaicorporation.gov.in/pgr`, which redirected to `https://gccservices.in/pgr` and displayed the complaint service during review. The registration form also loaded. The pilot replaces its older `erp.chennaicorporation.gov.in/pgr/` links; that older destination timed out during review. The same verified entry replaces the old link in the existing GCC road, encroachment, veterinary and vector-control records; their subject-specific contacts, scope and source links are preserved. This link maintenance does not expand the two-category language pilot. No complaint was submitted, no account was created, and delivery of email or phone responses was not tested.

EESL's disclosure publishes the consumer entry URL `https://support.eeslindia.org/`. That entry is retained rather than assuming an undocumented deep route. The review tool could not load the consumer portal, so its current interactive availability is unconfirmed; the published phone and email remain visible alternatives. The contact disclosure is an older document still available on the official site: the review date records this source check, not a fresh publication date or proof of service uptime.

Coimbatore was considered but not added. Keeping a nationwide location list does not establish nationwide verified service coverage.

## Guidance and translations

The English, Tamil and Hindi record content was authored and checked together for the same meaning, jurisdiction and limitations. These are the application's explanatory translations, not translations issued by the authorities. Official names, links, phone numbers and email addresses are preserved in the underlying verified records; localized contact objects change only their explanatory purpose.

`preparationAdvice` contains optional help: noting the street, landmark, observed duration and any safely available photograph or pole number. It is distinct from `requiredDetails`. GCC and EESL have no universal mandatory-document list in these records. Swachhata's photograph/location information is attributed to the government's app overview, with a direction to follow the current app's exact required fields. The guidance promises no fee, deadline, resolution time or automatic escalation outcome.

When editing these records, review the whole English/Tamil/Hindi set, confirm the authority's coverage, recheck each contact's own source, and update `lastVerifiedAt` and `reviewBy` only after that work. A source being reachable does not establish that every city or operator participates.
